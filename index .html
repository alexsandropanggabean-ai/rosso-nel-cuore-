RED VS BLUE

Charles Leclerc vs Max Verstappen

Two exceptional talents, two different paths, and a rivalry that Formula 1 fans love to watch.

Charles Leclerc represents Ferrari in scarlet red, while Max Verstappen represents Red Bull Racing. Their rivalry goes back to their karting days and continued into Formula 1.

In 2024, their battles carried extra meaning. Leclerc won his home race in Monaco, while Verstappen continued fighting for the championship. Different ambitions, one track, and plenty of moments that made fans hold their breath.
